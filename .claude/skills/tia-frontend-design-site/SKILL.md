# Tertiary Exams — Site Design System

**Load the user-level `tia-frontend-design` skill as well.** It owns the group's rules: no dead space or gaps, no chips, no small print above headings, no template layouts, an animated hero, generated images and a lighter theme by default. This file records how they apply on this site and wins on theme and look.

## Direction

A practice-exam storefront and exam runner for people preparing for certifications in Singapore. It should feel focused, credible and exam-ready, and let a signed-in user start an exam in one click.

## Theme

Light by default, with an optional dark mode the user can choose. Dark loads only if the user picked it (see `themeInitScript` in `src/app/layout.tsx`). Check every change in both. The owner asked for light blue with clear visual distinction in light mode, and a premium, modern blue in dark mode.

| Token (`src/app/globals.css`) | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `--c-page` | `#f4f8ff` | `#070f22` |
| `--c-ink` | `#0e1a33` | `#e9f0fb` |

Brand blue is `#1d5bf0`, defined in `tailwind.config.ts`. `.light-scope` keeps a block light inside dark mode. Any `bg-white` element with grey text needs its own `dark:bg-*`.

## Type

Archivo (display), IBM Plex Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono, loaded once in `src/app/layout.tsx`.

## Reusable pieces

- Classes: `.btn-*`, `.card`, `.input`, `.label` in `src/app/globals.css`. `.eyebrow` labels data only, such as a buy box; never put it above a heading.
- Product pages: `src/components/product/product-parts.tsx`. `ProductMeta` is the plain "Practice Exam · Anthropic · CCAR-F · Foundational" line that replaced the chip row.
- SEO copy, related Certifications and FAQs: `src/components/product/seo-copy.tsx` and `src/lib/product-faqs.ts`.

## Hero and images

- **Hero:** `src/components/hero-exam-visual.tsx`, an animated exam visual that freezes under `prefers-reduced-motion`. This is the site's benchmark; do not replace it with a static image.
- **H1:** "Practice Exams & Exam Vouchers in Singapore." The search phrase stays in the `h1`; "Prepare with confidence" sits on the line above.
- **Images:** `public/hero.webp` and `public/hero-dark.webp`. New images must be generated, not taken from a stock library, in both light and dark versions where they sit on the page background.
- **Social cards:** generated by `src/app/og/site/route.tsx` and `src/app/og/product/[slug]/route.tsx`.

## Navbar, footer and chat widget

- **Navbar:** `src/components/nav.tsx`. Admin search and notifications live in this top header, never in a side menu. Search works in place, with no pop-up dialog.
- **Footer:** `src/components/footer.tsx` and `footer-gate.tsx`. Four columns, no Shop section, no Practice Exam or Exam Voucher buttons under About, location in the Contact column.
- **Chat widget:** `src/components/whatsapp-widget.tsx`, whose mascot is a smiling support agent wearing our own green chat-bubble badge. It shows on the homepage, catalogs, product and `/p/` pages only, sits above the phone Buy bar, and rotates real offers: first at 5 seconds, then every 20 seconds, at most six per visit.

## Owner decisions on this site

- Practice Exam and Exam Voucher pages share one layout, with search rather than Vendor tags.
- The signed-in overview shows practice exams as cards that start an exam straight away.
- The exam summary is one column. Retake exam is on every summary and review page.
- GST inclusive is readable beside every price: `text-sm font-medium` at the smallest.
- Each sidebar entry has one job, with no duplicate pages.

## Site exceptions

- **Exam runner:** full-viewport test-centre screen, stays light in both themes. Its labels are fixed; see CLAUDE.md.

## Known violations

Do not copy these. Fix them when you touch the file.

- **Chips:** `src/components/exam-runner.tsx`, `src/components/user-menu.tsx`, the catalog cards' small labels in `src/components/storefront/`.
- **Rendered check:** sign-in height was tightened in code; confirm it fits at 1440×800 when you next touch those pages.
